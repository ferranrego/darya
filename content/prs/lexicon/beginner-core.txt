# Dari beginner core
#
# The words a learner needs on day one. Dari's ranking is anchored to the
# hand-curated Dari order rather than to a corpus, so it does not have
# Catalan's problem: of the concrete words probed, only 11 of 66 ranked past
# L1. What Dari has instead are gaps - 27 of them were missing from the
# lexicon entirely, including every day of the week, salt, potato, table,
# wall, shirt and yellow.
#
# Tagging a word `beginner-core` lets the first two levels teach it whatever
# its rank. It does not change freqRank and does not make the word count as
# already known.
#
# Kabul standard throughout: چوکی not صندلی, بوت not کفش, کچالو not سیب‌زمینی.
# One headword per line; anything the lexicon lacks is reported by
# scripts/tag-beginner-core.ts.
